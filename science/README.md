# Scientific account

The source can contain an entangled spin sector long before that sector carries a fixed teleportation advantage. The account separates this temperature effect from the quantum cooperation needed to access a strongly mixed singlet.

1. [Model](model.md) fixes the supplied state, the late unknown input, the all-outcome fidelity and the distinct laboratory partitions.
2. [Thermal spectrum](thermal-spectrum.md) derives the exact finite negativity and identifies its inherited NPT criterion.
3. [Teleportation bounds](teleportation-bounds.md) bounds every complete protocol and constructs an explicit receiver filter, retaining failed outcomes.
4. [Cooling and decoding](cooling-and-decoding.md) combines the converse with the known paired-helper decoder to obtain the matching fixed-score temperature order.
5. [Rare sectors](rare-sectors.md) proves the essential logarithmic suppression of the optimal global-receiver excess, with the order of limits explicit.
6. [Helper access](helper-access.md) gives the sharp singleton score and the fixed disjoint-pair budget converse.
7. [Interpretation](interpretation.md) connects the results through a finite example and the physical resource boundaries.

The spectrum, invariant-state framework and positive singlet teleportation endpoint are prior ingredients. [Related work](../references/related-work.md) gives their roles; [verification](../verification/README.md) separates finite calculations from proof.
